Isi bajet biasa kau. Fixed bills diasingkan awal pay cycle; belanja harian diagih sepanjang hari.

Data kau disimpan dalam browser ini sahaja.

Macam mana kami kira?

This app estimates your spending. It does not connect to your bank.

  1. Starting balance = take-home pay − all fixed bills.
  2. Current balance = starting balance − spending since your last payday. Logged days use your actual total. Days without entries use your daily budget. If you log today, only the rest of today uses an estimate.
  3. HP = current balance ÷ starting balance. HP stays between 0% and 100%.
  4. Money lasts = current balance ÷ daily spending. The run-out date assumes no extra income. Your next salary is not added to this forecast.
  5. What-if only reduces spending from now on. Cooking at home cuts your food budget by 50%. Coffee and smoking/leisure costs are cut completely.

Payday starts at midnight in your device's time zone. Days 29–31 move to the last day of shorter months. Bill money is set aside at the start of the cycle. The calendar shows due dates, not payment status. The chart spreads each logged day's total over its elapsed time; it does not track transaction times.

The live counter shows estimated spending, not real transactions. Actual amounts and times may differ.
