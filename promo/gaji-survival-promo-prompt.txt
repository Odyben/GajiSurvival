2. ABOUT THE PRODUCT (so the story is accurate)
==========================================================================
Gaji Survival estimates whether your salary lasts until the next payday.
- You enter take-home pay, payday date, monthly fixed bills and a daily
  spending budget.
- Fixed bills are set aside at the start of the pay cycle. Daily
  spending burns the rest.
- An RPG-style HP bar shows how much of this cycle's money is left.
- Shows spending per day, per hour and per minute, how many days the
  money lasts, and the exact date it runs out.
- A burn-down chart compares the current plan with a "what-if savings"
  plan.
- What-if toggles: "Skip kopi mahal", "Masak sendiri" (food budget
  -50%), "Pause smoking & leisure". They show extra days gained.
- "Boleh beli ke?": check a purchase's impact before buying.
- Also: expense log, bills and payday calendar, emergency-cost
  simulator, monthly recap, and a budget "receipt" image to share.
- Never connects to a bank. Data stays on the device. Works offline.
  Can be installed to the phone home screen.

6. MOTION LANGUAGE
==========================================================================
- Entrances: expo-out cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), 0.6-0.9s.
  Moves and morphs: cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1).
  Linear only for live tickers. No bounce or elastic, except one tiny
  overshoot on toggle knobs.
- Text reveals: lines rise from behind a clip mask (translateY 100% to
  0) while blur goes 8px to 0 and opacity 0 to 1. Words stagger
  50-70ms. Exits are faster than entrances (0.35s).
- Numbers: odometer-style rolling digits for balances, smooth
  count-ups for stats, and digits that never shift width.
- Camera: every shot has a slow, continuous push-in (scale 1.00 to
  1.035), plus gentle parallax between foreground UI and background.
- Focus pulls: when one element speaks, the others blur 4-6px and dim
  to 35%.
- Continuity: use match cuts and morphs instead of plain fades. The "?"
  becomes the logo pixels. The logo tile zooms into the dashboard. The
  lime toggle colour flows into the dashed savings line.
- Rhythm: cut on a 120 BPM grid (every 0.5s beat), with major scene
  changes on whole seconds, so music can be laid on later.
- Readability: hold each text card at least 1.2s + 0.25s per word. At
  most about 7 words of headline on screen at once (UI excluded).
- Texture: very subtle film grain (2-3% opacity, animated noise) and a
  soft vignette. Nothing more.
- AVOID: lens flares, 3D spinning logos, confetti, glitch effects, neon
  overload, emoji, busy gradients, drop-shadow stacks, cursor arrows,
  fake bank screens and stock photos.
